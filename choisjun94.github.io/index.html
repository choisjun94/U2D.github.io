04 / About

A planner working across disciplines.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Urban Policy and Administration, College of Urban Science, at Incheon National University. I direct the Urban Data Design (U2D) Lab.

My research focuses on urban informatics, urban AI, urban systems, and smart cities. I earned my master's and doctoral degrees in Community and Regional Planning from The University of Texas at Austin and am a certified planner in both the United States and South Korea.
